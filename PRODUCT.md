# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Designers, developers, hiring partners, and collaborators who want a concise view of David Dossett's current role, selected product work, and professional background.

## Product Purpose

Present David's work and experience with enough context to establish credibility quickly. Success means a visitor can understand what he does, where he has worked, and which projects define his practice without navigating a dense case-study archive.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, precise, and technically confident. The voice is direct and conversational, with the work carrying more weight than decorative presentation.

## Anti-references

Avoid oversized case-study imagery, identical card grids, decorative portfolio tropes, inflated marketing copy, and visual effects that compete with the content.

## Design Principles

- Put useful context before visual spectacle.
- Make the page easy to scan without flattening its hierarchy.
- Use restraint as evidence of judgment, not as an excuse for unfinished details.
- Keep interactions quiet, clear, and intentional.
- Preserve a personal voice in concise, specific copy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast expectations, preserve complete keyboard access, provide visible focus states, and respect reduced-motion preferences. Content and interaction states must remain understandable without relying on color alone.
